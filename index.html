Why this book exists

"A single well-chosen AI tool can compress hours of work into minutes — without asking you to become a professional writer, editor, or designer first."

— from the Introduction

Not a textbook. A field guide.

This book won't teach you the theory behind language models or diffusion-based image generators. Instead, it puts one hundred genuinely useful tools in front of you — organised by what you're actually trying to make: words, images, video, audio, slides, documents, social posts, research, workflows and income.

Every entry follows the same five-part format, so you can scan quickly and decide in seconds whether a tool deserves five minutes of your time:

  • What the tool does, in one line
  • How it fits into your everyday workflow
  • Its standout features
  • Who it's best suited for
  • A rough sense of pricing
